    <span class="eyebrow">The problem</span>
    <h2>Why this matters</h2>
    <p>A small organization stands up a WordPress site to tell its story and raise support. It's rarely patched, because no one's job is to patch it. Months later it's quietly exploited and starts serving malware or spam. Visitors get warned away, search engines flag it, and donors and partners start to wonder whether the organization can be trusted with anything at all.</p>
    <p>The technical fix is often straightforward. The damage to <em>confidence</em> is not — and for a mission-driven group, that reputation is everything. Larger organizations have staff for this. The smallest ones, doing some of the most important work, usually have no one.</p>

</section>
